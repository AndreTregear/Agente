Vision

Voice AI That Understands Latin America

Commercial ASR fails on regional accents, indigenous languages, and the messy reality of multilingual speech. We're building adapters and models that work for real people — starting with Quechua and Peruvian Spanish.

🗣️

Regional ASR Adapters

LoRA adapters on top of Qwen3-ASR that handle Peruvian Spanish accents, Quechua, and code-switching — the way people actually speak.

🏔️

Quechua First

7 million speakers across Peru, Bolivia, and Ecuador. Zero commercial ASR support. We're building the first open-source, production-quality Quechua speech recognition.

📱

Real-World Deployment

Built for noisy phone audio over WhatsApp. Deployed into agente.ceo (commerce) and agente.fit (health) — real products, real users in Peru.

🔓

Fully Open Source

Apache 2.0 licensed. Models on HuggingFace. Training code, data pipelines, eval scripts — everything public. Reproducibility is non-negotiable.

Roadmap

Development Phases

From regional ASR adapters to a full omni-modal voice AI — a step-by-step path to production.

Phase 1

Regional ASR Adapter

LoRA adapter for Quechua speech recognition on Qwen3-ASR. Peruvian Spanish accent handling. Training on ~336h of Quechua audio. Target: <20% WER, beating the 2022 SOTA of 22.75%.

🟢 In Progress
Phase 2

Audio Projector with Agentic Context

Regional audio embeddings fed directly into the LLM via a learned projector. The LLM sees both acoustic and semantic features — enabling context-aware ASR that understands domain (commerce vs. health) and user intent.

Planned
Phase 3

TTS Fine-tuning

Natural Peruvian Spanish voice synthesis. Regional prosody, natural rhythm, voice cloning for brand consistency across agente.ceo and agente.fit. Qwen3-TTS adapter training.

Planned
Phase 4

End-to-End Eval & Deployment

Full pipeline evaluation: ASR → LLM → TTS latency, WER on real phone audio, A/B testing against current Whisper + Kokoro stack. Production rollout to WhatsApp voice agents.

Planned
Phase 5

Full Omni Model Fine-tuning

When Qwen3.5-Omni releases — fine-tune the complete multimodal model with all our regional data. Audio-in, audio-out, single model. The endgame: a natively multilingual omni model that just works for Latin America.

Planned — Awaiting Qwen3.5-Omni
